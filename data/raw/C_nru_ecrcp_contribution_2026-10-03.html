Contribution

The Naoero Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Program offers a direct and impactful contribution pathway through the Naoero Treasury Fund, supporting both the investor and the nation's development. Contributions fund projects that drive economic growth and enhance climate resilience, including initiatives focused on infrastructure and economic diversification. This unique program allows participants to support Naoero’s sustainable future while advancing their global citizenship goals, contributing to the resilience of both Naoero and the broader Pacific region.

Program Update: Limited Time Offer

The Government of the Republic of Naoero has launched a limited time offer to celebrate the first anniversary of the program. A discount of USD 25,000 will apply to the contribution amount from 3 February 2026 for all current or new applications filed prior to 31 December 2026.

Contribution:

  • USD 90,000 for a Principal Applicant (limited time offer available until 31 December 2026)

  • USD 2,000 for each additional dependent, aged 16 and above

  • USD 15,000 for each sibling of a Principal Applicant or their Spouse

Application Fee:

  • USD 5,000 for a Principal Applicant

  • USD 2,000 for each eligible dependent

Due Diligence Fee:

  • USD 6,000 for a Principal Applicant

  • USD 3,000 for each additional dependent, aged 16 or above

  • USD 3,000 for a benefactor

Bank Due Diligence Fee and Transaction Charge:

  • USD 1,200 for a Principal Applicant

  • USD 1,700 for a Principal Applicant with up to three dependents

  • USD 2,200 for Principal Applicant with four or more dependents

  • USD 1,000 for a benefactor

Passport Fee:

  • USD 500 per passport

Processing Time

The program prides itself on a streamlined and efficient application process.

Total Processing Time:

  • Typically, three to four months from submission to citizenship approval

Transparent Process:

  • Clear communication and updates throughout your application journey

Expert Guidance:

  • Support from approved Agents at every step
